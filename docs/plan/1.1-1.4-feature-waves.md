## 1. Top 10 player complaints / risks

1. **Caves are pitch black and torches don’t exist.** Biggest atmosphere gap; blocks safe caving.
2. **Night is 20 minutes of hiding.** No bed, sleep or personal respawn.
3. **Nine-slot inventory.** No 27-slot main inventory; chests are the only storage.
4. **Autosave hitches.** 155 ms save at 2,025 columns; 11–85 ms tick spikes at 169–1,089 columns ([1.0.1 performance](1.0.1-performance.md)); save runs on the tick thread.
5. **Food is a scavenger hunt.** No farming, breeding or renewable food.
6. **Combat is click-spam.** Five mobs, zombies the only hostile, straight-line AI, no ranged/explosive threat, no attack charge.
7. **Everything looks the same.** 20 blocks, no glass/stairs/doors; structures only arrive in 1.1, so exploration has no destinations or loot yet.
8. **Iron is the end.** No XP, enchanting, trading or villagers.
9. **Public servers are cheat-friendly and unencrypted.** Client-authoritative movement, no TLS/auth, no speed/noclip validator.
10. **1.0 worlds may not open after an update.** Appending a block changes the v4 fingerprint and v4 is strict — 1.2 cannot ship until prefix acceptance lands.

## 3. 1.2 — Light & Home

**Goal.** “Torches make caves safe, a bed ends the night, wheat makes food renewable, and I can carry more than nine things.” This completes the first-day loop and is the most streamable update.

### 3.1 Specs

**Lighting.** Keep the `u16` RGB layout; base emitters are white (`R=G=B`). Add `emission:[u8;3]`, `attenuation:[u8;3]`, `sky_attenuation:u8` to `BlockType` (0–15, validated). Air 0/0, opaque 15, water 1, leaves 1, torch emission 14. **Sky seeding** runs top-down per column: `level = level.saturating_sub(sky_attenuation)`; opaque zeroes the column below. **Lateral propagation** is a 16-bucket 6-neighbour max-BFS, cost `max(1, attenuation)`.
**Edits** use **31³ box relight** (not full removal BFS in 1.2): recompute sky columns and block light in the box, seed from stored borders, batch all edits in a tick, budget ≤ 1 ms. Torch level 14 fits in 31 blocks. **Gameplay light** = `max(floor(sky × day_factor × weather_factor), max(R,G,B))`; use it for hostile spawn `≤ 7` (replaces day/depth), crops `≥ 9`, future fire/melt. A torch suppresses spawns in ~7 blocks.
**Mesher/shader:** `SectionSnapshot` (`render/src/mesh.rs:49`) captures sky+block light for the 18³ border; `Vertex.light: f32` becomes packed `sky:u8, block:u8` (32-byte stride); `shader.wgsl` computes `albedo × (block×tint + sky×daylight)`, with per-corner smooth light and AO. **Torch model:** non-solid `BlockShape::Cross`, two cut-out quads through the existing alpha path; floor-only, support break removes it. Wall torches/lanterns/coloured light are out.
**Migration:** 1.0 saves contain fake sky light. Add additive `world_meta.txt` with `light_version=2`; when absent, recompute each resident chunk’s light on load and write the marker. Without this, old caves never spawn mobs and torches look broken. Add a rolling `light_hash` to `World`/`Host::state_hash` (do not hash every dense section per call).

**Beds.** Blocks `bed_foot`/`bed_head`, two long in facing direction; full-cube collision (no partial shapes yet); both break together; no stored facing (pillow always +Z, documented). Use within 3.5 m in window **12,600..=23,400** or a thunderstorm; first use sets a personal respawn point (add an optional `respawn` line to `spawn.txt`). Multiplayer: all non-spectator players in the dimension must sleep (100%); fast-forward to next sunrise over 100 ticks; damage wakes; obstructed bed refuses. No anchors/explosions in 1.2.

**Farming.** Blocks `farmland_dry/wet`, `wheat_0..3` (cross cut-out, non-solid). Hoe converts grass/dirt (1 durability), seeds plant with air above and light ≥ 9. Growth is a host `CropState` block entity, not a random-tick system: **2,400 ticks/stage dry, 1,200 hydrated** (8/4 min for four stages), paused at light < 9. Hydrated = water within 4 blocks and ±1 Y, checked every 20 ticks (rain in 1.3). Harvest stage 3 = 1 wheat + 1–3 seeds; immature = 1 seed. Grass drops seeds at **12.5%**. Bread = 3 wheat, restores 5 hunger. Items: seeds/wheat/bread/3 hoes (`ToolKind::Hoe`, durability 59/131/250). No watering can, trampling, bonemeal or carrots in 1.2.

**36-slot inventory.** Slots hotbar `0..9`, main `9..36`, equipment `36..40`, window from `100`. `InventorySync` grows to 9+27 stacks; host/network player arrays grow; pickup fills hotbar then main, overflow drops. Client grid becomes 4 rows in both apps; old `inventory.txt` rows 0..8 remain valid. A 1.1 client cannot open a 1.2 world (unknown ids); protocol v3 rejects it clearly. Controls: all new interactions reuse the existing `Use` binding (hoe, plant, bed, trade, enchant); no new default keybinds, so the 17-action binding table and its tests stay unchanged.

**Save compatibility + pause.** Replace strict v4 equality with **prefix acceptance**: recompute `registry_fingerprint` over every prefix of the current registry; accept any matching prefix, log, still reject reorder/removal. O(30) hashes on load; keeps every 1.0.2 world open. Keep `world.obs` v4 in 1.2; a v5 manifest hash covering properties (SRV-D01) can wait for the block-state wave (1.5). **Async save:** snapshot dirty columns, encode zstd/CRC on rayon, write on a writer thread, keep the old save until commit, max one in flight; target tick impact **p99 ≤ 35 ms at 4,225 columns**. If cloning is too costly, reuse unchanged encoded chunks instead.

**Content delta.** 9 blocks (torch, farmland×2, wheat×4, bed×2), 6 items (seeds, wheat, bread, 3 hoes), ~14 textures; lighting, inventory, protocol and save slices are systems-only.

### 3.3 Acceptance

- Torch in a sealed 7×7×3 room: light 14 at source, 1 at 13 blocks; breaking removes it; no chunk-border seams.
- Edit→relight→remesh within 2 frames at camera / 100 ms at 8 chunks; relight ≤ 1 ms per edit.
- A y=20 cave in a 1.0.2 fixture is sky 0 after load; zombies spawn there but not within 7 blocks of a torch over 5 simulated minutes.
- Sleep at night advances to sunrise in 100 ticks, sets respawn, refuses while another multiplayer player is awake.
- Wheat reaches harvest in 4–5 min hydrated / 8–10 dry; yields wheat+seeds; bread restores 5 hunger.
- 36 slots survive save/reload and reconnect; overflow drops instead of deleting.
- A 1.0.2 v4 world loads after 1.2 block additions; a reordered pack is rejected.
- Autosave p99 ≤ 35 ms at 4,225 columns; host heap ≤ 350 MB after 10 min with torches/crops. If heap fails, switch to a monochrome light container (1 byte/block) or reduce dense sections before shipping.

## 4. 1.3 — Weather & Danger

**Goal.** “Storms roll in, lightning starts fires, night has real threats, animals breed, combat has timing, and structures hold loot.”

### 4.1 Specs

**Weather.** One machine, persisted in new `level.txt` v1 (phase, remaining, intensity, epoch); `tick` stays in `player.txt` but `level.txt` overrides once present. `Clear` 12,000–72,000 ticks; `Precipitating` 3,000–12,000; `Thunder` 3,600–12,000. At expiry rain→15% thunder/85% clear; thunder→30% rain/70% clear. Intensity ramps 1/200 per tick to 0.65–1.0/0.85–1.0. Determinism `splitmix64(seed ^ WEATHER_STREAM ^ epoch)`; epoch increments per transition; `do_weather_cycle=false` freezes.
Precipitation type per column from biome temperature (snow ≤ 0 °C, rain above, none dry). Rain hydrates farmland and extinguishes exposed fire; weather factor 0.8/0.6 in the light query.
**Client:** `Environment` gains weather phase/intensity (darker sky, shorter fog); new `render/src/particles.rs` draws rain/snow within 16 m (cap 2,000) plus splashes; particles never enter the host hash. `Sfx::RainLoop`/`Thunder` on the ambient bus, thunder delayed by distance/343 m·s⁻¹. **Lightning:** during thunder, `intensity/2,400` per tick; strike a resident column with sky access and precipitation; 10 HP within 3 m scaled by distance, ignite flammable if `do_fire_tick`, one flash and one thunder event.

**Fire.** `openblock:fire`: non-solid cross cut-out, emission 15, 1 HP/s to entities, burns out after 160 ticks. Every 40 ticks, 25% chance to ignite an adjacent flammable block (cap 64 fires); rain extinguishes; fires are transient and cleared on load. No fire charge/soul fire.

**Mobs.** **Skeleton** 20 HP, 3.5 m/s, keeps 6–12 m, arrow every 40 ticks (2 easy/3 normal, 20 m/s, gravity 28, range 16 m), needs line-of-sight and a server projectile; drops bones 0–2, arrows 0–2, 5 XP. **Creeper** 20 HP, 3.0 m/s, 30-tick fuse within 3 m, power 3 (~3.5 radius), ≤12 damage falloff, destroys blocks except bedrock, 30% loot, one `BlockEdits` batch + `Explosion` visual, one relight/remesh. **Spider** 16 HP, 4.0 m/s, 2 damage, neutral at light ≥ 9/hostile at ≤ 8, no climbing (documented), drops string, 5 XP. Use the 1.2 light spawn rule; raise hostile cap 8→12; no A* in 1.3.

**Breeding.** Feed two adults species food (wheat cow/sheep, carrot pig, seeds chicken): 600-tick love, child after 60, 600-tick parent cooldown, 6,000-tick growth (5 min scoped), 1–7 XP; full creature cap refuses birth and keeps cooldown; babies scale 0.5 and do not drop. `Mob` gains love/cooldown/growth/baby; append `mobs.txt` columns, tolerate old 8-column rows. New `InteractEntity { id }` message checks reach 3 m, item, cooldown. Carrot from loot/rare grass; if it slips, use wheat for pigs and document.

**Combat.** Charge `c = min(1000, elapsed_ms×1000/cooldown_ms)`; damage scale `0.2+0.8c²`; hit starts at `c ≥ 900`; sword 625 ms, hand/other 1,000 ms; crit 1.5× while falling and not sprinting. Server records `last_attack` and rejects early hits; client crosshair cooldown arc. Shields/sweep are 1.5.

**XP.** Add `xp_points`/`xp_level` to `Player`/`ServerPlayer`; curve `7+2L`, `37+5(L−15)`, `107+9(L−30)`. Award directly, no orb entity in 1.3: ores 1–4, smelting 0.1–1.0, mob kills 1–5, breeding 1–7. Death keeps XP in 1.3 (documented; death drops in 1.5). HUD level bar; persisted as appended `player.txt`/`network_players.txt` fields.

**Loot.** On first encounter of a chunk, if a 1.1 structure chest block has no `ContainerState`, fill deterministically from `splitmix64(seed,cx,cz,x,y,z)` using a small code table (bread, seeds, coal, iron, apple, string, arrow). Insert the chest record even if empty, so looting + reload cannot refill. Depends on the 1.1 structure hook; fallback is a one-time chest scan on chunk insert.

**Content delta.** Fire block; string/carrot/bone/arrow items; three mob visuals plus an arrow; rain/thunder SFX. Weather, XP, combat and loot logic are systems-only.

## 5. 1.4 — Company & Trust

**Goal.** “Villages have people to trade with, enchanting gives iron a future, and a public server is encrypted and does not trust client movement.”

### 5.1 Specs

**Villagers/trading.** `MobKind::Villager`, passive, persistent, no breeding; 2–4 per 1.1 village; persist profession/level/restock in `mobs.txt` appended columns. Two professions: **farmer** (wheat→emerald; emerald→bread) and **blacksmith** (coal→emerald; 3 emerald→iron axe); 3–5 offers, 2–4 uses, restock twice/day at composter/grindstone. No schedules/POI/nitwits; wander 8 m. Trade UI is a 3-slot window (input A/B/output) + offer list; server validates offer, uses, exact inputs, 6 m; reuse container revision/dupe checks. New `TradeList`/`TradeSelect`/`TradeMove`, protocol v5. Items `emerald`; workstation blocks `composter`/`grindstone` (recipes from planks/cobble).

**Enchanting.** `ItemStack.enchant: Option<(u8,u8)>`; persist as an appended token in inventory/equipment/items; network DTO optional fields; old lines parse as none. Five enchants: Efficiency I–V (+1.0 speed/level), Sharpness I–V (+0.5 HP/level), Protection I–IV (+4%/level), Unbreaking I–III (50%/level durability skip), Fortune I–III (+1 drop roll). Table block + UI: 3 offers from `splitmix64(seed,pos,player_level,offer_index)`, cost 1–3 levels + 1–3 lapis; one enchant/item, no anvil/removal. Add `lapis_ore` veins + `lapis_lazuli`; cut line substitutes coal and documents the deviation. Server validates levels/item, applies and consumes.

**TLS/auth.** Wrap the existing TCP JSON transport in **TLS 1.3 via rustls** (no QUIC yet). Config `tls_cert`/`tls_key`; if absent generate a self-signed cert under the world dir and print its fingerprint. Client `--tls` by default; `--insecure` LAN-only warning; plaintext loopback for tests. Password auth: argon2id hash in config, new `Auth` message before `Hello`, 5 attempts/10 s/IP, constant-time compare, disconnect on failure; whitelist after auth. TLS handshake gets its own 5 s deadline and per-IP admission cap. Document cert paths and the insecure warning; no save change.

**Movement validation.** Keep prediction; per `Move`: horizontal ≤ allowed state speed ×1.02 +0.05 m/s for 3 consecutive ticks, vertical ≤1.2× jump/terminal, no flight unless allowed, no solid penetration >1 mm. Accept ≤0.1 m error, rubber-band >1.0 m, reject + log repeated/noclip; send `MoveCorrection { pos, velocity }`. No full server physics/rewind in 1.4; document combat latency bias. A 2× speed bot is corrected within 3 ticks; normal play on 2% loss never rubber-bands.

**Content delta.** Emerald/lapis items, composter/grindstone blocks, villager visuals, five enchant tooltips. TLS/auth and movement validation are systems-only.

## 7. First three things to ship after 1.1

1. **Torches + real block/sky lighting** (with mesher/shader split and light migration). Biggest visible gap; makes caves playable and is the screenshot that sells the update.
2. **Beds + sleep + personal respawn.** Smallest effort for the largest QoL jump; turns the 20-minute night into a rhythm.
3. **Farming: farmland, wheat, seeds, hoe, bread.** Completes the food loop and is the dependency for breeding/trading.

Ship these as the 1.2 headline even if the 36-slot inventory lands a few days later in the same release. Do not hold the three for the inventory; run the inventory in parallel, because every later feature adds items.

## 8. Cross-cutting risks and sequencing rules

- **Save compatibility is the release gate.** Land prefix acceptance before the first new block id; test a real 1.0.2 fixture. `world_meta.txt`/light migration is equally mandatory.
- **Protocol discipline.** v3 (1.2), v4 (1.3), v5 (1.4); bump `ob_protocol` and `ob_core` together and the exact-version tests. Old clients get a readable `Disconnect`, not a JSON error.
- **Determinism.** Light, crops, weather, breeding and spawn rolls use `mix64`/`splitmix64` keyed by seed+tick+position; never wall clock or map order. Extend `state_hash` with `light_hash`, XP and crop state; update soak fixtures in the same PR.
- **Performance gates.** 512-block heap ≤ 350 MB; relight ≤ 1 ms/edit; autosave p99 ≤ 35 ms at 4,225 columns; weather + 2,000 particles + 12 mobs stays inside the frame budget. Measure headless where possible.
- **Two client paths are a tax.** Every UI feature must land in `lib.rs` and `net_app.rs`, or extract a shared HUD/inventory builder. Do not fork a third path; budget the duplication in client slices.
- **Content ids are append-only.** Append blocks after `openblock:chest`; items after the frozen catalogue; never reorder `MOB_DEFS`/`ToolKind`. Reordering is a save/protocol break.
- **No block states yet.** Crops/beds/torches use extra ids; keep the id count small. Redstone/stairs wait for the state layer rather than multiplying ids.
- **Bandwidth.** Light patches and 36-slot sync are small, but crop growth broadcasts can be frequent; coalesce edits per tick and cap the outbound queue by bytes, not frames (SRV-D04).
- **Authority.** 1.2 farming/sleep validates reach/state server-side; 1.4 movement is an envelope, not full physics. Do not claim anti-cheat completeness in release notes.

## 9. Implementation order (Lead briefing)

1. Week 1: L (light/save prefix/migration), P (protocol v3 schema), R (content ids/textures/mesher contract) in parallel. No content append before prefix is green.
2. Week 1–2: S after L/R; C after P; F after S or after extracting `persistence.rs`.
3. Week 2: integrate light-on-edit, sleep/time sync, 36-slot loopback; fix state-hash fixtures; run 32-chunk heap and autosave measurements.
4. 1.3: W+E+M together after 1.2 light merge; B after M’s entity schema; K after the 1.1 structure hook; P v4 first.
5. 1.4: N first (`ItemStack`), then V; T/D parallel with separate modules; P v5 last.

## Open questions

- Should 1.2 ship the 36-slot inventory and protocol v3 together with lighting, or split them if the client slice overruns? (Current recommendation: ship the Light & Home headline first, inventory in a 1.2.x patch if needed.)
- Do we keep the current two client UI paths (single-player `App` and `NetApp`) for 1.2, or extract a shared inventory/container view first? The plan budgets the duplication but extraction would pay off by 1.3.
- Is a full block-state/save-v5 format worth doing before redstone/dimensions, or continue appending blocks with prefix acceptance until 1.5?
- The 512-block far LOD hides deep caves; should far detail be user-configurable in 1.2, and at what default?
- Which structure-loot/chest initialization path is acceptable: a worldgen descriptor sidecar or server-side registration on chunk stream?
