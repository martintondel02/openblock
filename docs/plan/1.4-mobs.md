# 1.4 danger slice — mobs, combat, breeding, XP and structure loot

> Status: implemented in the `1.4-dev` gameplay wave · Last updated: 2026-11-25
> Owner slice: `crates/content`, `crates/sim`, `crates/server`, `crates/protocol`,
> `crates/worldgen`, `crates/client` (HUD/inventory/net only; **no render edits**).
> Follows [1.1–1.4 feature waves](1.1-1.4-feature-waves.md) §4 and the 1.2 light
> engine. Weather, fire spread and lightning from §4 remain out of this slice.

## 1. Player promise

Night is dangerous in three distinct ways, animals are renewable, combat rewards
timing, XP persists, and exploring a 1.1 structure is worth the trip because its
chest has deterministic loot.

## 2. Mob catalogue

All stats live in `ob_content::MOB_DEFS` (`MobSpec`). Kinds append after the 1.0
catalogue; indices never move.

| Kind | HP | Speed | Hitbox | Attack | Drops | XP |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| cow / pig / sheep / chicken | 10/10/8/4 | 1.4–1.5 | 0.9×1.3 … 0.4×0.7 | — | existing food/leather | 1 |
| zombie | 20 | 2.2 | 0.6×1.8 | melee 3 HP, 1.6 m, 1.0 s cooldown, 0.5 s wind-up; burns in daylight | — | 3 |
| spider | 16 | 4.0 | 1.0×0.9 | melee 2 HP, 1.7 m, 1.0 s cooldown, 0.4 s wind-up; leaps 2–6 m; climbs 1-block steps; neutral at gameplay light ≥ 9 until hurt; does not burn | 1 string | 5 |
| skeleton | 20 | 3.5 | 0.6×1.8 | ranged 3 HP, keeps 6–12 m, 2.0 s cooldown, needs line of sight; burns in daylight | 1 bone + 1 arrow | 5 |
| creeper | 20 | 3.0 | 0.6×1.8 | 1.5 s fuse within 3 m, radius 3.5 m, ≤12 HP falloff; does not burn | 1 gunpowder | 5 |

Deterministic details:

- AI decisions use `mix64(kind, quantised position/age, purpose salt)`; there is
  no mutable RNG. Projectiles integrate gravity **28 m/s²**, arrow speed
  **20 m/s**, lifetime 4 s, and raycast every ≤0.4 m to prevent tunnelling.
- Skeleton aim solves the ballistic arc (`t = horizontal/20`,
  `vy = dy/t + ½·g·t`) so arrows hit a stationary target at the stated range.
- A spider leap is a 6 m/s vertical hop plus 1.5× horizontal speed, 2 s cooldown.
  Climbing raises the AABB by 1.001 m and retries the horizontal sweep; it reverts
  cleanly if the raised box collides.
- Melee attacks require reach **and line of sight**; a wind-up starts once and the
  hit lands only if the player is still in reach and visible.
- Creeper explosions remove at most **192 blocks**, protect **bedrock, water,
  chests, furnaces and beds**, clear the crop map, and spawn **no block drops**
  (documented cut line). They damage every mob/player inside the radius with
  linear falloff `1 - distance/(radius + 0.5)`.

## 3. Spawning

- Hostile cap **12**; passive cap **10**; breeding adds a local cap of **6
  passive animals within 24 m**.
- Hostiles need gameplay light **≤ 7** (`max(floor(sky·day·weather), block RGB)`),
  two air cells and a solid floor. Night surface spawns are 16–48 m away; cave
  spawns within ±16 m vertically of a player below Y=40. Passive animals need a
  grass-topped surface column 12–40 m away.
- Kind weights are zombie 5/16, spider 4/16, skeleton 4/16, creeper 3/16.
  Candidate positions, kind and variant are deterministic hashes of the world
  seed, tick and attempt. Spawns are never within 8 m of the player and a torch
  (light 14, radius ~7) suppresses them.
- Creative players are not targetable, so no hostile spawns are anchored to
  them; there is no separate peaceful setting in 1.4.
- Bred animals are `persistent`: they skip age and 64 m distance despawn (void
  despawn still applies). Wild mobs keep the 600 s/64 m rules.

## 4. Combat

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Sword swing cooldown | 625 ms |
| Hand / non-sword cooldown | 1,000 ms |
| Charge `c` | `min(1, elapsed_ms / cooldown_ms)` |
| Minimum hit charge | 0.90 |
| Damage scale | `base × (0.2 + 0.8c²)` |
| Critical | ×1.5 while falling (`!on_ground && vy < -0.1`) and not sprinting |
| Base hand damage | 1 HP |
| Base sword damage | wood 4, stone 5, iron 6 HP |
| Armor | existing formula: `4%` per defense point, 80% cap, 1 durability/piece per hit |

Early swings return `AttackResult { hit:false, cooldown_remaining_ms }` and do not
wear a tool. The client gates its own send, shows a crosshair cooldown bar and a
mob name/health bar, and mirrors the server's remaining cooldown after every
result. Critical hits are detected for the internal single-player player; network
movement is still client-authoritative in v4, so a network attacker's fall state
is not trusted and network crits are disabled until movement validation (1.4
trust wave). Mob melee has a wind-up, so a player can retreat before the hit.

## 5. Breeding and growth

- Food: **wheat** for cow, sheep and pig; **seeds** for chicken. Carrots are a
  cut line (no carrot item yet).
- First feed consumes one item in survival (creative keeps it), sets
  `love_timer = 30 s` and is accepted even without a partner.
- Feeding a second in-love adult of the same species within **8 m** clears both
  love timers, sets `breed_cooldown = 30 s` on both, schedules a **60-tick**
  birth and awards **1–7 XP** (`1 + mix64(parent ids) % 7`).
- The baby is 0.5× scale, inherits one parent's colour variant, starts
  `growth_timer = 300 s` (6,000 ticks), never drops loot and cannot breed until
  grown.
- Rejections use stable reasons: `no_target`, `dead`, `too_far` (>3 m),
  `not_breedable`, `baby`, `cooldown`, `love_active`, `wrong_food`, `not_food`,
  `cap`.
- The client shows a love/birth text toast (and the server relays a short chat
  line in multiplayer); no heart particles are required.

## 6. XP

- Formula (same as the planned curve): next level `7+2L` for `L≤14`,
  `37+5(L−15)` for `15≤L≤29`, `107+9(L−30)` above 30. Cumulative to level 30 is
  exactly **1,395 points**.
- Sources in this slice: mob kills (table above) and breeding (1–7). Ores,
  smelting and enchanting remain future work. XP is awarded directly; no orb
  entity is simulated or replicated in 1.4.
- Persisted in `player.txt` (fields 11–12) and `network_players.txt` (trailing
  `xp<points>:<level>` token). Death/respawn keeps XP (1.3 rule); no XP drops yet.
- Both clients draw a green XP bar with the current level just above the hotbar.

## 7. Items and structure loot

New items append after the 1.2 catalogue: `openblock:bone`, `string`,
`gunpowder`, `arrow` (ids 67–70). All stack to 64 and have procedural client
icons/tints. `arrow` is crafted from **1 stick + 1 string → 4 arrows**
(flint/feathers intentionally cut).

Worldgen writes a chest into every structure kind; `structure_chests(&instance)`
is the single source of truth used by both the builder and the server. Loot is
rolled from `STRUCTURE_LOOT_TABLE` with **4–6 rolls**:

| Entry | Count | Weight |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| bread | 1–2 | 3 |
| wheat | 1–3 | 3 |
| seeds | 1–3 | 2 |
| iron ingot | 1–2 | 2 |
| coal | 1–4 | 3 |
| torch | 2–4 | 2 |
| string | 1–2 | 2 |
| bone | 1–2 | 2 |
| apple | 1–2 | 1 |
| arrow | 4–8 | 2 |

The roll mixes the world seed, the structure region hash and the chest position.
When a structure chunk streams in, the host registers a `ContainerState` only if
the chest block exists and no record is present. The record is saved even when
empty, so looting and reloading can never refill it. Existing container open,
transfer, persistence, revision and stale-window checks are reused.

## 8. Protocol v4

`PROTOCOL_VERSION = 4` in `ob_protocol` and `ob_core`; v1–v3 peers are rejected
with `protocol_mismatch: server protocol 4, ...; 1.3 and older clients must
upgrade to a 1.4+ build`.

Added/changed messages:

- `client → server`: `InteractEntity { id }` (server validates 3 m reach, food,
  cooldown and caps).
- `server → client`: `XpSync { points, level, progress, required }`,
  `InteractResult { id, accepted, reason }`, `Explosion { pos, radius }`.
- `AttackResult` gains `charge`, `critical`, `cooldown_remaining_ms`.
- `EntityKind::Projectile { projectile }`; `EntitySnapshot`/`EntitySpawn` gain
  `variant` and `baby`; `EntityStateEntry` gains both fields.
- `EntitySpawn` is used for arrows; arrows are removed by the normal entity
  interest diff or an immediate despawn when hit/expired.

JSON/TCP framing, the 2 MiB frame cap and chunk/light messages are unchanged.
Loopback tests cover the new messages, version rejection and reconnect.

| `mobs.txt` | appended `variant love cooldown growth persistent` (13 fields; 8-field rows still load) |
| `network_players.txt` | trailing `xp<points>:<level>` token |
| `block_entities.txt` | unchanged format; structure chests are ordinary `chest` rows, including empty ones |
| `world.obs` | unchanged; chest blocks come from the 1.1 structure writer |

AI intent, velocity, wind-up/fuse timers, arrows and pending births are transient
and restart from idle/full cooldown after a load, matching the existing mob
policy.

## 11. Remaining visual/audio work (render/audio owners)

No render-crate edits were made. The following polish is deferred and currently
represented by text/HUD cues or plain entity cubes:

1. Creeper flash/fuse swell, explosion smoke/flash and camera shake.
2. Critical-hit/heart/love/XP orb particles (XP is direct; no orb entity exists).
3. Proper arrow mesh/fletching, skeleton bow and spider leg animation.
4. Skeleton bow-draw and creeper hiss/fuse audio; explosion one-shot audio.
5. Mob name tags and smooth health bars could move from primitive UI to the
   renderer later.

## 12. Risks and cut lines

- **Network crits** are disabled until movement validation; documented above.
- **Explosion block drops** are disabled to keep duplication and item-spam risk
  low; creeper gunpowder and entity loot still drop.
- **Persistent bred mobs** bypass distance/age despawn; the global cap (10) still
  bounds them, and a large ranch can block natural passive spawns.
- **Loot determinism** depends on world seed and worldgen hash; changing either
  changes chest contents. Treat both as stable once a world is shipped.
- **Protocol v4** rejects 1.3 clients outright; there is no compatibility shim.

## Open questions

- How soon can the render/audio layer add creeper swell/flash, explosion smoke/flash/camera shake and hiss/boom SFX (the mechanics are server-authoritative already)?
- Are text/primitives enough for XP and breeding feedback until particles/orbs exist, or should XP orbs be a 1.4.x visual follow-up?
- Should network critical hits stay disabled until server-side movement validation lands (currently client-authoritative movement), or is a conservative envelope acceptable sooner?
- Should explosions drop blocks (with anti-dupe/rate rules) instead of deleting them, and which blocks must remain protected?
- Do we want mob pathfinding before 1.5, or are LOS-only chase and jump/climb rules acceptable for the current threat design?
- Should loot be re-rolled with a loot-table version, or is seed+worldgen-hash determinism enough for existing worlds?
